# Use cases
| Who | Situation | What to do in IdleWatt | What to take away |
|---|---|---|---|
| FinOps lead | Quarterly bill review | Overview -> Machines (filter mostly unused) -> Action plan CSV | A ranked list with dollar impact |
| Platform engineer | Planning a migration | Upload your fleet CSV -> Servers load map | How many servers you really need and where the headroom is |
| Dev/test owner | Idle environments | Quality tab -> Off-hours scheduling | Machines to switch off nights and weekends |
| Sustainability lead | Carbon reporting | Report tab -> Download | kWh and CO2 estimates with assumptions stated |
| Risk-averse CTO | "How safe is this?" | Trade-offs -> pick Cautious; Quality -> coverage | The risk you accept, in numbers |
| Researcher/student | Study a method | docs/METHODOLOGY.md + tests | A transparent, reproducible pipeline |
## Walk-through
1. Open the app: the headline says how many servers and dollars can be saved. 2. Click a finding to see the machines behind it. 3. Try Cautious/Balanced/Bold, save two scenarios and compare them in Trade-offs. 4. Check Quality before trusting the numbers. 5. Download the report for stakeholders and the action plan for engineers.
